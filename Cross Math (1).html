Number bank

How to play: tap a number in the bank, then tap an empty square to place it. Tap a filled square to send its number back. Work left to right and top to bottom, ignoring the usual order of operations. Result squares turn green when an equation works and red when it doesn't. Any arrangement that makes all six equations true counts as solved.
